# Blog Drafts & Research Notes

Drop research notes here (one file per topic). Ask Claude to turn a draft
into a finished post — it will be written to `_posts/` following the
"Blog Writing Rules" in `CLAUDE.md`.

These files are NOT published as blog posts and are blocked from public
view on the website (see `_redirects`). Still, never put patient
information in this folder.

## Template

Copy this into a new file, e.g. `_drafts/perimenopause-sleep.md`:

```
TOPIC: [e.g. Perimenopause and sleep]
WHO IT'S FOR: [e.g. women 40-55 in Baltimore County]
KEY POINTS I WANT TO MAKE: [your own pharmacist opinion, in your words]
RESEARCH (from OpenEvidence/Doximity):
- [fact or statistic] — [PubMed or journal link]
- [fact or statistic] — [PubMed or journal link]
SERVICE TO MENTION: [e.g. hormone testing kits, birth control consult]
```

## Research tips
- Ask OpenEvidence/Doximity narrow questions that have a number for an
  answer, and always ask for the citation (journal, year, PubMed link).
- Click every citation once to confirm it opens the real paper.
- No link = no statistic in the post.
